Neighborhood Services Co-Op

Community services that you own with your neighborhood.

Our Mission

To deploy community-owned services into the neighborhood that might otherwise be unavailable or out of reach. To benefit residents, bring the community toghether, and reduce costs. We can reclaim and repurpose our neighborhood’s abandoned storefronts as community-owned hubs that provide or house those essential services, foster local creativity, and ensure that the resources of our community stay in our community. Our mission is to build a vibrant, safe, and cooperative space where neighbors share tools, technology, and support.

How it works

A Co-operative Co-Op

Neighbors share ownership, make decisions together, and build the services we all agree the community needs.

Community ownership

A community-owned and operated space that democratically provides services and co-owns tools, as well as facilitating important community collaboration.

Democratic decisions

Members have a voice in finances, budgets, membership criteria, planning, and the services the co-op provides. All planning and documentation is available for public review.

Many ways to take part

Membership can include different levels of support, from sweat equity to limited access. It should benefit the whole neighborhood, not just its members.

Run by a board representative of different areas of the neighborhood and supported by local businesses, the aim is to collectively decide on services to provide, how to provide them, and work together to make those services happen.
